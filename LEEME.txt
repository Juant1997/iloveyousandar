ROMANTIC PAGE - INSTRUCTIONS

1. Unzip the folder and open index.html in your browser.
2. The navigation works like this:
   index.html -> "Yes" button -> regalo.html -> tap the gift five times -> recuerdos.html
   "No" button -> terminado.html

CHANGING THE PHOTOS
Place six images in assets/fotos/ using these exact names:
- foto-1.jpg
- foto-2.jpg
- foto-3.jpg
- foto-4.jpg
- foto-5.jpg
- foto-6.jpg

You can use .png or .webp, but if you change the extension you must also update the
corresponding src in recuerdos.html. The design crops images to preserve the collage
composition; portrait photos work best.

ADDING BACKGROUND MUSIC
Place a local audio file at assets/audio/romantic-song.mp3. The music button on
recuerdos.html will play and pause it. Browsers may prevent automatic playback, so
the visitor must press the button to start the song.

CHANGING THE SAD STICKER
Place your sticker at assets/stickers/triste.png. If it does not exist, 🥺 will be shown.

PERSONALIZING
- Change "Sandar" and the text directly in the HTML files.
- Colors, sizes, layout, and styles are in css/styles.css.
- Bootstrap 5.3 is loaded from a CDN, so you need an internet connection for its styles.
- The gallery adapts to small screens by rearranging the collage for mobile.
